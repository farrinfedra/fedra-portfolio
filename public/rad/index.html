01Method

A representationat every step

Standard diffusion models only learn to denoise; whatever semantics they pick up stay buried inside the network. RAD gives the model a compact latent representation ut at every step: it predicts ut from the noisy state xt, then denoises with it. These latents are learned by aligning them with an external representation model (external alignment) or with the model's own features (self-supervised, internal alignment).

The representation is compressed into one token and added back into the denoiser at the same block.

xtℓ ← xtℓ + MLP(ut)

Denoise

The remaining SiT blocks predict the velocity, now conditioned on the representation.

The model, step by step

  1. 1

    Generative process Eq. 1–2

    Each step samples a representation first, then a denoising step consistent with it.

    Averaging over ut makes every transition a continuous mixture of Gaussians. It is more expressive than one Gaussian, which is why RAD tolerates large steps.

  2. 2

    Noising process Eq. 3, 5

    While training, ut is a noised copy of the clean image's features from a frozen encoder, on the same schedule as xt: coarse at high noise, sharp at low noise. It sees what the generator cannot, so it acts as a teacher.

  3. 3

    Variational bound Eq. 4

    A conditional VAE at every step: rebuild the cleaner state from ut, and keep the model's own ut close to the teacher's. The KL is an information bottleneck.

    Proof sketch
    • Use the noising process as the variational distribution and apply Jensen's inequality.
    • By Markov + Bayes, ∏ q(x_t | x_t−1) telescopes, so each step's terms group together.
    • What remains (posterior entropies and the prior KL) depends only on the noising process: a constant in θ, ϕ.
  4. 4

    Plug in Gaussians App. A.3.2

    Same covariance on both sides, so the KL is just a distance between means: REPA's alignment loss, derived instead of assumed.

    With a velocity network the denoising term becomes a weighted velocity regression. Set wt = 1 as usual and you get the loss below.

THE LOSS a weighted variational lower bound on log p(x0)
DenoisingThe usual velocity loss, except the denoiser also sees the representation ut.
AlignmentPulls the predicted representation toward the target features (here a frozen encoder). It falls out of the bound as a KL term, not a heuristic add-on.

In practice the weight SNR(t)/2β², which blows up as t → 0, is replaced by its bounded form λt = αt²/(αt² + β²σt²) (× 0.5), with t ~ U(0,1) and β = 1.

KEY INSIGHT

In representation alignment, representations are learned but then discarded: they are never used for generation. Remove ut from RAD's denoiser and these methods, such as REPA, become special cases.

Architecture

Training in practice

Inject early

Block ℓ = 2 for B/2 and ℓ = 8 for L/2, XL/2, inside the first third of the network. The most influential architectural choice.

B/2: block 2 → FID 8.2 · block 8 → 15.1

Use a flow policy

Replace the policy's Gaussian noise with a conditional normalizing flow (4 affine couplings, zero-initialised, so training starts Gaussian).

FID 10.3 → 8.7

Normalize · compress · modulate

Remove 60% of the token mean (γ = 0.6) and rescale to unit size; an MLP over tokens compresses N tokens to one; adaLN-style modulation, zero-initialised.

FID 8.7 → 8.2

Bounded alignment weight

Use the saturated weight λt × 0.5 instead of SNR(t)/2β², plus adaptive per-sample weighting with p = 1.

FID 8.2 → 7.7 (with early injection)

Tune β only at sampling

Train with β = 1; sample with βinf ∈ [0.5, 0.9] to concentrate the policy, trading diversity for fidelity without retraining.

best βinf: 0.7 (B/2), 0.8 (L/2, XL/2)

Small overhead

The control pathway is the same size at every scale; most of its cost is the projector, which REPA also trains but throws away.

05Interpolation

A smoothlatent space

Take two real ImageNet images, read their representations with RAD's own policy head, and blend them with a weight m. RAD morphs one subject into the other while pose, viewpoint and framing stay put.

1
Encode

From each real image read ū = hϕ(xtc, tc) at a small noise level, and invert it to noise x1 with the ODE.

2
Blend

ūm = m ūA + (1 − m) ūB; class embeddings blend the same way; the noises are slerped.

3
Sample

Generate from the blended noise with the blended representation: 50 Euler ODE steps, no guidance, no external encoder.

m = 0.50

Representation, inverted noise and class embedding are interpolated at the same m · 50 Euler ODE steps.

Compared with REPA baboon → macaque

REPA-XL/2, trained 10× longer (4M iterations), jumps between poses and scenes mid-way. RAD-XL/2 at 400K changes the subject gradually.
